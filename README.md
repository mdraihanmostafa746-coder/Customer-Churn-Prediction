## Business Problem

Customer churn is an important challenge for telecom companies because losing customers can affect recurring revenue and increase the cost of acquiring new customers.

The objective of this project is to identify customers who are at higher risk of churning by using their demographic, service usage, contract, and billing information.

The project will also investigate which customer characteristics and service-related factors are associated with churn. These insights can help a telecom company identify at-risk customers and support data-driven customer retention strategies.
